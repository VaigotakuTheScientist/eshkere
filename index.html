The thesis

One foundation. Infinite games.

Health

The non-optional foundation

Everyone has a right to health — the conditions and needs underlying physical, mental and social flourishing. Sleep, movement, nourishment, connection, a mind that works. Health is serious because nobody can opt out of needing it, and everything else stands on top of it.

Games

Everything you choose on top

The optional ways people pursue enjoyment, achievement, relationships, exploration, creativity, knowledge, identity and meaning. Optional by definition — and where almost everything people cherish about being alive actually lives.

Games depend on health. That is why the foundation comes first — and why it is never the whole point.

The stakes

The floor is not secure.

For a great many people it never has been. Health is lost to poverty, illness, violence, isolation and neglect — and every game those people might have chosen is lost with it. That is a present fact, not an abstraction.

A floor that holds today can also be undermined tomorrow. Some of the plausible ways are global rather than local — how powerful AI systems are built and governed among them, alongside conflict, pandemics and ecological damage.

So health first is not only a private ordering. Where the foundation is missing or threatened, securing it is a public good — making the floor real for people beyond yourself, and keeping it standing for the people who come next.

Which of these problems Eshkere is actually positioned to help with is still an open question.

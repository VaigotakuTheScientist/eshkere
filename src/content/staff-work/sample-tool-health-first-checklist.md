*This is a sample tool entry — a concept published to demonstrate the
format. The tool it describes has not been built.*

If Eshkere ever ships small tools, they should look like this: narrow,
honest, and aimed at the foundation. The concept here is a one-page
checklist to run before committing to any new project or game:

- What does this pursuit cost in sleep, movement, food and connection?
- Is that cost a brief, chosen loan — or an open-ended default?
- What is the repayment plan, and when does it trigger?
- Would I still choose this game if it never impressed anyone?

Whether this becomes a real interactive tool, a printable page, or nothing
at all is one of the many things Eshkere has not decided yet.

The first real Eshkere project is the one you are looking at. This website
is a statically generated prototype built to do three things honestly:

1. **State the philosophy.** *Maximize Health, Play Games* — health as the
   non-optional foundation, games as everything worth choosing on top of it.
2. **Build the structure.** A portfolio system, an editorial research
   section, a blog, team and careers pages, a media center, working search —
   the skeleton a real organization would grow into.
3. **Refuse to fake the content.** Where nothing real exists yet, the site
   shows deliberate empty states or clearly labelled samples instead of
   invented staff, partners or achievements.

The site is built with Astro and TypeScript, generates fully static output,
and stores every piece of content in editable collections so real material
can replace the placeholders without redesigning anything.

Vadym started Eshkere around a single ordering: health — physical, mental and
social — is the non-optional foundation of a life, and everything layered on
top of it is a game worth choosing deliberately and playing wholeheartedly.

Eshkere is deliberately early. Rather than announce an organization that
doesn't exist yet, the work right now is figuring out what form the idea
deserves — a company, a fund, a studio, a community, or something without a
clean name — and building the thesis in public while that question stays
open.

Vadym also maintains [Eshkere Strategy](https://vaigotakuthescientist.github.io/eshkere-strategy/),
a separate personal strategy for building power and directing it toward AI
safety. The two projects share a name and a person, not a mission.

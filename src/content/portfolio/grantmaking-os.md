Grantmaking usually starts with a funding area and ends with a decision, and
the distance between the two is where most of the work actually happens.
Grantmaking OS is the working system Eshkere uses to cross it: map the
domain, find where it is actually bottlenecked, evaluate what could be done
about that, coordinate the people and resources a plan needs, execute, and
feed what happened back into the map.

It is published as a Notion template, so the structure can be copied and
adapted rather than described.

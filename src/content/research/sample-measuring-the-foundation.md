*This is a sample research entry — a working note published to demonstrate
the format, not a finished piece of research. It contains questions, not
findings.*

If health is the foundation, an organization built on that claim needs some
way of knowing whether the foundation is getting stronger. This note collects
the questions such a measurement approach would have to answer — it does not
answer them.

## Questions a serious approach must face

- Physical, mental and social well-being move on different timescales. What
  does progress even look like across all three at once?
- Self-reported flourishing and observable health diverge. Which do you
  trust when they disagree?
- Measurement changes behavior. How do you watch the foundation without
  turning the watching into another anxious game?

## Why publish questions

Publishing the open questions first is deliberate. It sets a standard for
what real research entries here will owe readers: stated uncertainty,
before stated conclusions.

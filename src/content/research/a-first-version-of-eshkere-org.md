Eshkere now has a website. This is genuinely all the announcement claims: a
first public prototype exists, and you are reading it.

The site sets out the working philosophy — **Maximize Health, Play Games** —
and builds the structure a real organization would need: a portfolio, an
editorial research section, a blog, a team page, a careers page, a media
center. Most of that structure is deliberately empty.

## What is real here

- The philosophy, as far as it has been developed. [Our Approach](/our-approach)
  is the most complete statement of it.
- The visual identity and this website itself.
- The honest empty states. Where you see "taking shape" or "forthcoming",
  that is the truth: nothing exists there yet.

## What is not real

Anything labelled **Sample** is placeholder content demonstrating a format.
Eshkere has no staff roster, no funded portfolio, no partnerships and no
publication record to announce. When those things exist, they will replace
the samples — not quietly, but as real announcements like this one.

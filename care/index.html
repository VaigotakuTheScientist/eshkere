Hair routine map

Detangle with care, choose a wash rhythm and play with shape.

Morning

When you get ready

  1. 1

    Check texture and tangles

    Core

    Look for tangles and see how your hair is sitting.

    Why this step? Check texture and tangles

    Preference A quick check helps you decide whether to leave your hair alone, detangle it or refresh the shape.

  2. 2

    Refresh the shape

    Optional

    Lightly dampen sections and reshape with your fingers.

    Why this step? Refresh the shape

    Preference Try this if you prefer a tidier shape between washes. How well it works depends on your texture.

Evening

Before bed

  1. 1

    Loosen a tight style

    As needed

    Release styles that pull or feel uncomfortable.

    Why this step? Loosen a tight style

    Health guidance Repeated tension can damage hair. Loosening a tight style reduces pulling.

    AAD · Hairstyle tension

Periodic

On wash day

  1. 1

    Wash the scalp

    Core

    Massage shampoo into your scalp, then rinse.

    Why this step? Wash the scalp

    Health guidance Shampoo lifts oil and product buildup. Concentrating it on the scalp avoids unnecessary washing along the lengths.

    Supplies Shampoo

    AAD · Healthy hair tips

  2. 2

    Condition the lengths

    Core

    Apply conditioner where your hair needs slip, then rinse.

    Why this step? Condition the lengths

    Health guidance Conditioner helps smooth strands and makes them easier to detangle.

    Supplies Rinse-out conditioner

    AAD · Healthy hair tips

  3. 3

    Blot and dry gently

    Core

    Blot with a soft towel and air-dry or use low heat.

    Why this step? Blot and dry gently

    Health guidance Blotting avoids rough rubbing. Less heat exposure helps limit damage.

    Supplies Soft towel or T-shirt

    AAD · Healthy hair tips

As needed

When tangles get in the way

  1. 1

    Detangle from the ends

    As needed

    Use a wide-tooth comb gently, starting at the ends.

    Why this step? Detangle from the ends

    Health guidance Working up gradually helps loosen knots without pulling through them. Dampness and conditioner may help, depending on your texture.

    Supplies Wide-tooth comb

    Take care Pause if the comb catches or your scalp hurts. How wet to work depends on your hair texture.

    AAD · Healthy hair tips

Ask a dermatologist about persistent scalp irritation or unexpected hair loss.

Nails routine map

Care for your hands and nails, with room to try a finish you like.

Morning

After washing your hands

  1. 1

    Dry around the nails

    Core

    Dry your fingertips and around the nails after washing.

    Why this step? Dry around the nails

    Health guidance Keeping nails clean and dry is a basic part of nail hygiene.

    AAD · Healthy nail tips

Evening

After your last hand wash

  1. 1

    Moisturize hands and nails

    Core

    Massage a hand moisturizer around the nails and cuticles.

    Why this step? Moisturize hands and nails

    Health guidance Moisturizing after washing helps with dryness.

    Supplies Hand moisturizer

    AAD · Healthy nail tips

Periodic

When you want to trim or polish

  1. 1

    Trim and smooth the edges

    As needed

    Trim fingernails almost straight across and file snags.

    Why this step? Trim and smooth the edges

    Health guidance Smooth edges are less likely to catch on fabric.

    Supplies Nail clippers, Emery board

    Take care Leave cuticles intact. Avoid cutting or forcing them back.

    AAD · Healthy nail tips

  2. 2

    Try a clear or colored finish

    Optional

    For ordinary polish, follow the label for base, color or clear polish, and top coat.

    Why this step? Try a clear or colored finish

    Preference The finish is a style choice. Compare what you like on your own hands.

    Supplies Regular polish and coats

    Take care Use ordinary polish as directed and stop if it irritates your skin.

As needed

When you want to remove polish

  1. 1

    Remove regular polish

    As needed

    Follow the remover label, then moisturize.

    Why this step? Remove regular polish

    Health guidance Polish remover can dry out the nails. Moisturizing afterward helps with dryness.

    Supplies Regular polish remover, Hand moisturizer

    Take care Avoid scraping or peeling the polish off.

    AAD · Manicure safety

Ask a dermatologist about unexplained nail changes, swelling or pain.

Face routine map

Start with gentle cleansing, moisturizer and sun protection.

Morning

Before heading out

  1. 1

    Cleanse gently

    Core

    Use a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water. Pat dry.

    Why this step? Cleanse gently

    Health guidance Gentle washing removes surface buildup. Scrubbing can irritate the skin.

    Supplies Gentle cleanser, Soft face towel

    AAD · Face washing

  2. 2

    Apply moisturizer

    Core

    Smooth moisturizer over skin that is still slightly damp.

    Why this step? Apply moisturizer

    Health guidance Moisturizer helps hold water in the skin.

    Supplies Face moisturizer

    AAD · Everyday skin care

  3. 3

    Apply sunscreen

    Core

    Before going outdoors, use broad-spectrum, water-resistant SPF 30 or higher on exposed skin.

    Why this step? Apply sunscreen

    Health guidance Sunscreen helps protect against ultraviolet rays. Include the ears and neck if they are exposed.

    Supplies Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen

    Take care Follow label directions and use shade or protective clothing as well.

    AAD · Applying sunscreen

Evening

At the end of the day

  1. 1

    Wash off the day

    Core

    Cleanse gently to remove sunscreen and daily buildup.

    Why this step? Wash off the day

    Health guidance Cleansing removes surface buildup without the friction of scrubbing.

    Supplies Gentle cleanser, Soft face towel

    AAD · Face washing

  2. 2

    Moisturize after washing

    Core

    Apply moisturizer while your skin is slightly damp.

    Why this step? Moisturize after washing

    Health guidance Applying moisturizer to damp skin helps retain moisture.

    Supplies Face moisturizer

    AAD · Everyday skin care

Periodic

Between daily routines

There are no additional scheduled steps in this starter routine.

As needed

During time outdoors

  1. 1

    Reapply sunscreen outdoors

    As needed

    Reapply about every two hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating.

    Why this step? Reapply sunscreen outdoors

    Health guidance Sunscreen wears off over time and can wash away with water or sweat.

    Supplies Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen

    AAD · Applying sunscreen

Ask a dermatologist about persistent irritation or a specific skin concern.
